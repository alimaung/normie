# Instructions for Completion - T00221
**Issue: 2024-08**

## 5 Description

### 5.1 Introduction
During the approval process of a consumable material request (AfTS), an assessment of the material intended to be used shall be performed.

It shall be assured that for the application, maintenance, storage and disposal of the product, the Health and Safety and the environment protection is not compromised. The restrictions for the product application, storage and disposal shall be indicated.

Fundamentally, it shall be assured that a material does not in any way compromise the safety of engine parts. This can be relevant for material strength, corrosion protection etc. The manufacturing laboratory function may set requirements for a material or refer to established specifications, which are then passed onto the supplier when the product is ordered.

This is a digital process. Further information on how the process will be carried out digitally can be found in RRTI00032.

### 5.2 Request for a new product
If a new material is to be used in production, the requestor shall first file a search in:

**OMat Catalogue, MLC 104 and RRD Consumables Catalogue**

for comparable already approved materials.

The current state of the requests can also be found in the RRD Consumables Catalogue.

### 5.3 Instructions for Completion

#### 5.3.1 General
The AfTS form (T00221) is available in RRD Templates & Vorlagen. This instruction for completion is embedded in the AfTS form.

In the original form, the field numbers are identified by numbers printed in italics.

**Field 1: Request No.**
The Standardisation Department shall allocate a unique identification number here, upon receipt of the AfTS.
Example: 001/2022 (number consisting of the consecutive number of the request and the year).

#### 5.3.2 Applicant
The following fields shall be completed by the applicant.

*Note: More information given by the applicant in this section results in an easier / quicker approval. Please provide as much information as possible.*

**Field 2: Name / Date / Department / Phone**
This field shall be populated by the name, department initials, date of preparation and phone number of the applicant.

**Field 3: Designation**
The applicant shall enter the unique name for the consumable material. This is found in the SDS or the TDS.

**Field 4: External Part Number**
The supplier / manufacturer part number (if available) uniquely identifying the consumable material, shall be entered here.

**Field 5: Identification of the Request**
Here, it shall be indicated if this AfTS is a new AfTS (New Demand) or a change of an existing AfTS (Change in Demand).

For a change to an existing AfTS, the previous AfTS No. shall be indicated in Field 20, and the reason for the change shall be given in Field 19.

**Field 6: Product classification**
Here, it shall be indicated if the material is a part (abrasives, tapes, PPE, etc.) or a compound (chemicals).

**Field 7: REACh-Code**
The PC, PROC, & ERC Codes according to REACh shall be indicated here (if applicable and known – see Attachment 1 to 3).

**Field 8: Supplier**
The supplier is chosen by the purchasing department. Normally no entry by the applicant is necessary here.

**Field 9: Manufacturer**
The manufacturer of the material shall be entered here.

**Field 10: Purpose, Substantiation, Process description, form of utilization**
A brief explanation of what the material is, why the material is required, and how the material will be used/applied (including examples) shall be written here. The procedure which documents the use of the consumable material shall also be stated (if possible).

**Field 11: Engine Programme**
The specific engine programme(s) for which the material is going to be used shall be entered here.

**Field 12: Place of Use / Site**
The specific area of use – with department, area (building/floor/room) and work site (DW or OU) – shall be entered here.

The Team Leads (names/ emails) of the relevant Department regarding introduction/ change is to be entered here.

**Field 13: Product relevance (Is there contact with aviation parts?)**
Here it shall be indicated whether the material is used on aviation parts (OEM or MRO engines).

If the material does not have any contact with aerospace parts, then the AfTS does not require approval by the Manufacturing Laboratory. Shelf-life information can be found in the TDS.

**Field 14: Usage**
Here it shall be indicated whether the material will be for short-term or long-term use.

**Field 15: Stock holding**
Here it shall be indicated if the procurement and inventory administration will be handled by the warehouse management and if the material is available to order in SAP.

**Field 16: Base unit of measure SAP: (Size, Weight, Unit of Supply)**
The desired form of supply shall be specified here (e.g., gram, litre, piece etc.) – meaning the unit of measure in which stocks of the material are managed, and which can be supplied by the manufacturer/supplier.

For consumables, auxiliary and operating materials that are included in a parts list (adhesive, lacquers etc.) the quantity unit of measure according to the parts list shall be indicated.

**Field 17: Demand per month**
The demand per month (as the multiple of the base unit of measure), frequency of use and the amount required for a single use shall be entered here.

**Field 18: Enclosures**
The documentation enclosed shall be listed here. All relevant documents, especially the SDS and the Technical Data Sheet shall be sent together with the AfTS.

For all consumable materials, a product data sheet and a current EU Safety Data Sheet (no older than 2 years) in German shall be included (according to § 5 Gefahrstoffverordnung). If the material is already listed in the RRD Consumables Catalogue, and an SDS fulfilling these requirements is already available, then this existing SDS may be used. If an SDS is not available, the purchasing department shall assist in requesting the SDS from the supplier.

For all consumable materials used on aviation parts, an approval by Rolls-Royce (in reference to RRMS30031 and/or OMat), or a specification (MSRR, CSS, OMat etc.), or other customer document which controls the material or its application shall be indicated on the AfTS. If multiple specifications or documents apply, then all of them shall be listed. It is not required to attach a copy of the specification to the AfTS, unless the controlling material specification is a customer document (e.g., a Bombardier specification).

**Field 19: Explanations**
Additional information or specifications for the assessment of the material shall be entered here.

For changes or updates to an existing AfTS, information or context about the change shall also be given here.

**Field 20: Reference to past requests**
Approved AfTS for the same material shall be referenced here.

**Field 21: Desired date for the application of the product**
The planned date of use for the material shall be entered here. An average of approx. 3 weeks shall be allowed for the approval of an AfTS. This corresponds to a fully completed AfTS form with all necessary enclosures (see Field 18) attached to it.

#### 5.3.3 ChemScan
The following fields are related to the ChemScan evaluation of the material.
Completion of "ChemScan Part B" is only necessary when the material is delivered in a kit with two separate parts.

**Field 22: Name / Signature**
Hazardous Substances Management shall indicate the results of the ChemScan check here.

**Field 23: Result of evaluation for Health and Safety**
Hazardous Substances Management shall indicate the occupational health and safety guidelines that are relevant to the material.

**Field 24: Result of evaluation for environment protection**
Hazardous Substances Management shall indicate the environmental protection guidelines that are relevant to the material.

#### 5.3.4 Environmental Protection Officer
The following fields are related to the environmental protection evaluation of the material.

**Field 25: Name / Signature**
The Environmental Protection Officer shall indicate if, and when the check has been accomplished.

**Field 26: Result of evaluation for Environmental Protection**
It shall be indicated here if the application of the material according to the Environmental Protection Officer is approved, not approved or the approval is subject to restrictions.

**Field 27: BImSch approval required?**
The Environmental Protection Officer shall indicate if BlmSch approval is required here.

**Field 28: AwSV system required?**
The Environmental Protection Officer shall indicate if AwSV system is required here.

**Field 29: Involvement of environmental protection in risk assessment required?**
The Environmental Protection Officer shall indicate if environmental protection requirements should be recorded in the risk assessment.

**Field 30: Consider joint storage?**
The environmental protection officer shall indicate whether and how the material can be stored together with other substances (quantity restrictions?).

**Field 31: Explanations**
This block may be used for further information on the approval (e.g., approval restrictions, reason for no approval etc.). Additionally, notes for the goods receipt / warehouse may be indicated here.

#### 5.3.5 Occupational Health & Safety
The following fields are related to the Occupational Health & Safety evaluation of the material.

**Field 32: Name / Signature**
This field shall be used by the HS&E department to sign for the processing of the AfTS (see approval information in Field 33) according to Health and Safety.

**Field 33: Result of evaluation for Occupational Health & Safety**
It shall be indicated here if the application of the material according to Health and Safety is approved, not approved or the approval is subject to restrictions.

**Field 34: Product is HS&E-relevant?**
It shall be indicated here whether the material is HS&E-relevant.

**Field 35: Information for storage required?**
It shall be indicated here whether the storage warehouse should be informed about the approval of the material at the end of the process.

**Field 36: Hazardous substance-specific risk assessment for workplace and usage required?**
It shall be indicated here if a hazardous substance risk assessment shall be created for the application of a HS&E-relevant material, specific to usage in the workplace.

**Field 37: Operating Instruction required?**
It shall be indicated here whether an operating instruction shall be created by the user for the application of a HS&E-relevant material.

**Field 38: Explanations**
This block may be used for further information on the approval. Additionally, notes for the goods receipt / warehouse may be indicated here.

If the material does not have any contact with aerospace parts, then the AfTS does not require approval by the Manufacturing Laboratory.

#### 5.3.6 Manufacturing Laboratory
The fields 39 through 49 shall be completed by the Manufacturing Laboratory. Field 39 shall be completed by standardisation if a Manufacturing Laboratory is not required.

Manufacturing Laboratory approval is not required for materials that do not have contact with aerospace parts.

**Field 39: Product and Supplier approval**
The applicable box shall be ticked:

Product approval "is necessary, but not yet available" shall be ticked for the first-time order of a material (new material), or when any of the following changes are made to a previous AfTS:
- New manufacturer
- New type of application (method / use)

If this box is ticked, Fields 40-46 shall be filled out next.

Product approval "is necessary and available" shall be ticked for any of the following changes to a previous AfTS:
- New scope of work (new area or site / new programme)
- Change of base unit of measurement

In this case, there are no changes to the product or supplier requirements, and the previous AfTS shall be extended to a new area. If this box is ticked, Fields 41, 43-45 and 47-49 shall be filled out next.

Product approval "is necessary and available" shall be ticked once the first-time order of this AfTS has already taken place. If this box is ticked, Fields 47-49 shall be filled out next (Fields 41, 43-45 should already be filled out).

Product approval "is not possible" shall be ticked, and state a reason why shall be stated, if the AfTS is rejected.

Product approval "is not required" shall be ticked if the material does not have any contact with aerospace parts (Field 13). In this case, the AfTS skips to the Standardisation signature / approval step.

**Field 40: Name and signature**
This field shall be used by the Manufacturing Laboratory to sign for the approval of a first-time order for product approval.

**Field 41: Shelf-life when purchased**
The shelf-life requirement shall be entered here. Shelf-life information can be found in the TDS. This requirement shall be entered in the SAP Quality Agreement.

When there is no shelf-life limit for the material, or no impairment to the material's features can be expected with proper long-term storage conditions, "keine Einschränkung" shall be ticked.

**Field 42: Certificate (according to DIN EN 10204)**
The appropriate certificate shall be indicated in this field. This requirement shall be entered in the SAP Quality Agreement.

Here, a 3.1 Certificate shall be chosen if there are any test requirements (batch or type tests) stated in the relevant specification for the material.

A 2.1 Certificate shall be chosen if there are no test requirements in the relevant specification for the material. A 2.1 Certificate shall also be selected for materials that are already listed in MLC104 / OMat and only have type test requirements.

A 2.1 Certificate shall be chosen if all type testing will be done in-house or by an external laboratory (if only some of the testing can be done in-house, a 3.1 Certificate must be chosen to cover the remaining tests). A RR test report shall be submitted to the manufacturing laboratory and reviewed with the manufacturer's certificates. External or In-house testing is only permitted for type testing (e.g., water break testing), batch tests must be covered by a 3.1 Certificate.

**Field 43: Entry into MLC104 required?**
Here it shall be stated if entry into MLC104 is required, or if the product is already listed.

**Field 44: Entry into OMat required?**
Here it shall be stated if entry into OMat is required, or if the product is already listed.

**Field 45: Product approval**
This field shall be used to enter the material specifications for the consumable product. Materials are assessed according to relevant specifications (e.g., RRMS, MSRR, CSS, etc.) or customer requirements, respectively. A copy of the relevant specification may be attached to the AfTS at this point. This requirement shall be entered in the SAP Quality Agreement.

**Field 46: Approval requirements for the purchase order text**
This field shall be used to enter the product, manufacturer, and supply chain approval requirements. This requirement shall be entered in the SAP Quality Agreement.

**Field 47: Name and signature**
This field shall be used by the Manufacturing Laboratory to sign for the approval of the material for order and use.

Approval for use shall only be given once the certificates from the first order have been checked, and the product and manufacturer listed in MLC104 and/or OMat, if required.

**Field 48: Certificate (according to DIN EN 10204)**
The appropriate certificate shall be indicated in this field. This requirement shall be entered in the SAP Quality Agreement. Here, a 3.1 Certificate shall only be chosen if there are batch testing requirements stated in the relevant specification for the material. Otherwise, a 2.1 Certificate shall be chosen.

**Field 49: Approval requirements for the purchase order text**
This field shall be used to enter the product, manufacturer, and supply chain approval requirements. Materials shall be assessed according to relevant specifications (e.g., RRMS, MSRR, CSS, etc.) or customer requirements, respectively. This requirement shall be entered in the SAP Quality Agreement.

#### 5.3.7 Standardisation
The following fields shall be completed by the Standards Office.

**Field 50: Name / Signature**
This field shall be filled out with a name, signature and date, to indicate that the request for the consumable is closed.

**Field 51: Part Number**
A part number shall be entered here. A new TKZ shall be created:

- For products that have never been ordered or known products with a new manufacturer, if the composition (ingredients, etc.) is different.
- For products that have an old TKZ that starts with "1000", this TKZ could have been created for English or American work and has now been overwritten and used for a completely different product.
- For products that have an old TKZ, where, for example, there is no tab for "Quality Management" in SAP. This decision is made after consultation with purchasing and H&B Stoffe RR DW.
- If a product that has already been released is requested in a different measurement (delivery container, shape, size, etc.).
- If a product is delivered as a KIT (e.g., base + hardener) a single TKZ will be assigned, otherwise separate AfTS must be evaluated with different TKZs.
- For orders that are not purchased through SAP, such as for gloves, or protective goggles, in different sizes. A single TKZ shall initially be assigned to the product in general but, as soon as the product is to be ordered via SAP, a TKZ shall be created for each size that can be delivered.
- For the same product, for each different colour (e.g., paint (marking colours) or marking pens).
- For the same product for a different location if the laboratory requirements are not the same.

**Field 52: Explanations**
This field may be used for explanations or extra information e.g., alteration.
