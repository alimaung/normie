Working with Revisions

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

Note: Only users with the appropriate permissions can view the publication and do rollback or release on the publication revision draft using the functions under the Publication Operations button in the header bar.

When releasing a publication in Data Manager, the publication can be released as a draft or released automatically by checking the Auto Release checkbox. If the publication was auto released and the release was successful, the Publication Operations button is not visible.

If the publication is a draft and you have the permission to do so, you can perform these operations on the revision:
  • Release a Publication Revision
  • Rollback the revision
  • Reload a Publication Revision
