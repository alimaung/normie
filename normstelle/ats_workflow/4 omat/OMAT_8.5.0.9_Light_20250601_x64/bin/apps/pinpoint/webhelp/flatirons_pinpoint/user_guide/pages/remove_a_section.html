Delete Section

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

The organization can choose one of the below options to delete the section.
  • Permanent Deletion
  • Deletion in Progress

Permanent Deletion

Deleting a section removes it permanently from the document. Before deleting a section, you should remove all attachments, annotations, and references to it or move those to a different section. Once you delete the section, these elements will no longer display correctly.

  1. Enter authoring mode. Refer to Enter Authoring Mode.
  2. In the left margin of the Content pane, click the Edit content within this section icon for the section you want to delete. The selected section is available.
    For example:


    Authoring Tab - Section Open to Delete

  3. Click Delete Section in the Authoring Toolbar to delete the section.
    A confirmation message appears:

  4. Click the button to delete the section.
    A success message appears:

Deletion in Progress

Deleting a section or part of a section shows a strike-through in the deleted text. The user can see and help identify the deleted texts.

  1. Enter authoring mode. Refer to Enter Authoring Mode.
  2. In the left margin of the Content pane, click the Edit content within this section icon for the section you want to delete.
  3. Click Save.
