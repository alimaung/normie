Navigate Search Results in the Reference Tab

The Reference tab search results shows a history of opened wiring diagrams and ESPM user guides.

  1. Perform a wiring search Perform a Wiring Search.
  2. On the Wire tab, click the Wire Part Number hyperlink in the Connection column. For details, refer to Navigate Search Results in the Wire Tab.
    Shows the Reference tab results.


    Reference Tab Results

  3. Perform a FIN search Perform a FIN, Zone, or Panel Search.
  4. Input a known good FIN in the area and click on Search button
    Shows the Select one business category page.
  5. Click on the Wire List.
    Shows the FIN tab information.
  6. Click on the highlighted area in the FIN tab.
    Shows the Wire tab information.
  7. Click on any line of the Wire tab information.
    Shows the Technical Data tab information.
  8. Click on the Wire Part Number hyperlink on the Technical Data page.
    Shows the Reference tab results.


    Reference Tab Results for FIN Search
