Shopping Basket Options

The option for the shopping basket available for your organization depends on configuration. If not configured, these options are not available in your environment.

These are the possible options for the shopping basket:

  • Shopping Basket - Parts Order List: This option allows you to add parts to a list for reference when ordering parts.
  • Shopping Basket - Parts Ordering: This option allows you to immediately order the part through a third-party application for your organization.
  • Parts Inquiry: This option allows you to perform a part inquiry and pass parameters for the part to a third-party application for your organization.
