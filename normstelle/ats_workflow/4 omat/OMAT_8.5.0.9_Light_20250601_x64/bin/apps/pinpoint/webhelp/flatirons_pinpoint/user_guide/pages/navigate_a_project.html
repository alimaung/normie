Navigate a Project

The Library pane contains one or more projects/fleets, represented by icons.

  1. Click on a project to find the publication you are looking for.
    Publications are represented by icons.
  2. Select the publication you want to view.
    The Library pane is now minimized, and the publication structure is displayed in the Table of Content panel below.
    Note: You can bring the Library back to maximum size by clicking on it.
    Note: For some publication types you will be redirected to the Filter page before you can start viewing the content.
  3. Browse the publication by clicking on the chapters in the Table Of Content. When you click on an entry, it expands to reveal lower levels, such as sections, tasks, subtasks, and subjects.
    Note: To avoid multiple expanded entries in the Table of Content pane, Pinpoint automatically collapses the previous chapter when a new chapter is selected.
  4. Click on the document you want to view (represented by a icon).

    A loading indicator is displayed before the document appears in the Content pane.

    Illustrations in the document are represented by links. Click on these links to view illustrations in the Graphic pane.
