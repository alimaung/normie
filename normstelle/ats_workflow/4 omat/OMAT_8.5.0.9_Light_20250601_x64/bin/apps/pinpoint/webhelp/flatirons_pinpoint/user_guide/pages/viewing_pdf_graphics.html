Viewing PDF Graphics

Pinpoint supports the display of PDF documents as media. When a publication has PDF as media (starts with ICN), the PDF is displayed as a figure link. Clicking the link displays the PDF in the Graphic pane.
Note: A PDF thumbnail is generated based on the first page of the PDF document.
The display of a PDF with 3D content depends on the browser:
  • Internet Explorer - the display of 3D content is supported with Acrobat PDF Reader installed.
  • Chrome and Firefox - the 3D content is displayed as a 2D image.

These steps describe how to open and view the PDF:

  1. Open Pinpoint and navigate to a document that contains a PDF as media.
  2. Click on the PDF figure link to open it in the Graphic pane.
    The PDF opens in the Graphic pane.


    PDF Graphic in the Graphic Pane

  3. Click the icon in the Graphics pane to display a list of the graphics in the document.
    The list of graphics appears, allowing you to select a graphic.
    Note: For Internet Explorer browsers only: When you click the icon, the PDF graphic is hidden and the list of graphics appears. After you select a graphic from the list, the list closes, and the selected PDF graphic is displayed.
