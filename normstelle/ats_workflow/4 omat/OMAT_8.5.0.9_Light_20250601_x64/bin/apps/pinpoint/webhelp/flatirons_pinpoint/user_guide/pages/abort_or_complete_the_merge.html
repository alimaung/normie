Abort or Complete the Merge

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

Users must have permissions for the Can merge new revisions privilege to view a merge report and resolve conflicts.

After opening a merge report, you have the option of aborting or completing the merge.
Note: Completing the merge requires that all conflicts are resolved first. Refer to Resolve Revision Conflicts for information on resolving conflicts.
Open the merge report, and select one of these options:
  • To abort the merge, click the button.

    A confirmation message appears:

    Click the button to confirm.

  • To complete the merge, click the button.
    Note: This option becomes available when there are no documents with conflicts remaining in the merge report.

    A confirmation message appears:

    Click the button to complete the merge.

    When the merge is complete, the preview (right-side) version of all the documents in the merge report becomes the approved, live version. This is the version that users will see in the Pinpoint Viewer.
