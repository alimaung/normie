Insert Warning/Caution/Note

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

  1. Enter authoring mode. Refer to Enter Authoring Mode.
  2. Access the section where you want to add a warning/caution/note.
  3. In the Authoring Toolbar, click the down arrow next to the Insert warning and caution icon.
    The drop-down menu appears.


    Drop-down Menu

    • From the drop-down menu, select the option you want to add.
    • Enter the text in the box.
