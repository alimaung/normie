Log Out

For security purposes, you should log out after you are finished using the application. This prevents others from accessing the information.
  1. At the top right corner of the application, click the More button.
  2. From the drop-down menu, click the button.
You are logged out of the software. The login page appears.
Note:

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

If you are logged in as a guest and the capability for guest users to switch accounts is enabled, you will see the button in the More drop-down menu. Clicking this button logs you out of the public environment and displays the Access Control login page.

If allowed, your administrator can enable the ability to switch accounts. Administrators, refer to "Enable Guest Access for Pinpoint" in the Flatirons Pinpoint Configuration Guide.
