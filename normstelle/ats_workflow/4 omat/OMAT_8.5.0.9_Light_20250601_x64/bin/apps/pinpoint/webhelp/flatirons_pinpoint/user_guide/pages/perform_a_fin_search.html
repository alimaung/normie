Perform a FIN, Zone, or Panel Search

Note: The FIN/Zone/Panel Search page is only available for A350 S1000D data.
You can start a FIN search either from the in the header bar or from the FIN/Zone/Panel Search pane directly. If you start the search from the header bar, you will be directed to the FIN/Zone/Panel Search pane, and the search text is inserted in the FIN field.
Note: The FIN search option is available in the Search bar in the header bar only when you select a publication that allows for FIN searches.

The placeholder text in the Search box corresponds to the type of search you are currently doing. For example, if you are searching a library, the text "Search the Library" is shown in the Search bar.



Fin/Zone/Panel Search page

These steps are based on starting the search in the FIN/Zone/Panel Search pane.

  1. Select a publication that allows a FIN search.
  2. Open the FIN/Zone/Panel Search pane.
    Note: You can also enter the text you want to search for in the Search bar in the Action Bar, and press Enter to open the FIN/Zone/Panel Search pane directly.
  3. Do one of these choices:
    • Enter a full or a partial FIN in the FIN field.
    • Enter a full or partial zone number in the Zone field.
    • Enter a full or a partial panel number in the Panel field.
      Note: You are not allowed to search in more than one category at a time.
  4. In the Business Category drop-down list, select ALL or a specific category.
  5. Click the Search button.
  6. If ALL was selected, the business categories and the number of hits are first presented in a Select one business Category dialog.


  7. Choose the category you want to see search results for.
  8. The search results are presented below:


    Search Results Example

    Navigate the search results by clicking on entries. How search results behave when they are opened depends on the business category:
    • AIPC: Presents the document in a new AIPC tab in the Content pane.
    • AMM: Presents the document in a new AMM tab in the Content pane.
    • ASM: Presents drawings/schematics in the Graphic pane.
    • AWM: Presents wiring diagrams in the Graphic pane. For the A350 the FIN search results show a publication title in the Publication column. See the search results below:


      FIN Search Results with Publication Title

    • TSM: Presents the document in a new TSM tab in the Content pane.
    • CB List: Search results are presented in a C/B tab with a list of FINs and their titles. When a FIN is selected in the list, information about the FIN is presented in a Technical Data tab.
    • Fin List: Search results are presented in a FIN tab with a list of FINs and their titles. When a FIN is selected in the list, information about the FIN is presented in a Technical Data tab.
    • Panel List: Search results are presented in a Panel tab with a table of panel numbers with columns showing Type, Access To, Access To FIN, Zone and Applicability. When a panel row is selected, information about the panel is presented in a Technical Data tab. If you click the “Zone” number, a pop-up appears with information about the selected zone.
    • Wire List: Search results are presented in a FIN tab with a list of FINs and their titles. When a FIN is selected in the list, information about the wiring related to this FIN is presented in a Wiring tab. Notice that the FIN you selected will be marked in bold letters to make it easier to identify it in the Wiring tab. You can further select a row in the Wiring tab to view detailed information about the wire in a Technical Data tab. In the FIN search results when there are wires connected to the FIN such as a plug. There should be a link called "Wire List" that can be clicked to show a list of wires for this FIN. See the search results below:


      FIN Search Results with Wire List

    • Zone List: Search results are presented in a Zone tab with a table of zone numbers with columns showing Designation, Applicability and links to relevant lists. For each row entry, you have these options:
      • To view information about the zone in the Technical Data tab, click anywhere in the row that does not have a hyperlink. Then you can open related zoning illustrations by clicking links in the REF. FIGURE column of the Technical Data tab.
      • To open the FIN List tab with a list of all FINs within the zone, click the row’s FIN List link.
      • To open the Panel List tab with a list of all panels within the zone, click the row’s Panel List link.
      • To open the Wire List tab with a list of all wires within the zone, click the row’s Wire List link. The first column in the Wire List tab is Wire number followed by Connector details. When clicking a wire number, Wire Details appears. When clicking a wire search link, the corresponding wire number opens in the Wiring search.
    Note: When searching using the "ALL" category, you can easily switch business category in the drop-down list in the search result header.

    From the Search Results area, click the Print Search Results icon to print the search results in PDF format.

  9. If a specific business category was selected, the search results for this category only is presented below.
