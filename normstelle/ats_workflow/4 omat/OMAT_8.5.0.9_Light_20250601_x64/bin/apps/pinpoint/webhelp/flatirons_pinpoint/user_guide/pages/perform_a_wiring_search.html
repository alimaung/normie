Perform a Wiring Search

The Wiring Search pane is available when you open a supported wiring publication in the Library.

From the Search Types menu, select one of these menu options:
  • To search electrical paths, click the Electrical Path menu option. Refer to Search Electrical Paths.
  • To search equipment lists, click the Equipment Search menu option. Refer to Search Equipment Lists.
  • To search hookup lists, click the Hookup Search menu option. Refer to Search Hookup Lists.
  • To search wire lists, click the Wire Search menu option. Refer to Search Wire Lists.
  • To search splice lists, click the Splice Search menu option. Refer to Search Splice Lists.
  • To search bracket lists, click the Bracket Search menu option. Refer to Search Bracket Lists.
  • To search ground lists, click the Ground Search menu option. Refer to Search Ground Lists.
  • To search terminal lists, click the Terminal Search menu option. Refer to Search Terminal Lists.
  • To search circuit breaker lists, click the Circuit Breaker Search menu option. Refer to Search Circuit Breaker Lists.
