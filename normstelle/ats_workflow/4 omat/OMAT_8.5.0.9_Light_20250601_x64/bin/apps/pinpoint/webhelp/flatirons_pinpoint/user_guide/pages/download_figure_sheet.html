Download Sheet

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

All users can download figure sheets in the Figure List. If users do not have permissions to author, the Download Sheet is the only icon available.

To download a figure sheet, do these steps:

  1. Enter authoring mode. Refer to Enter Authoring Mode.
  2. From the Content pane header, select the Figure List icon.
    The Figure List dialog appears. For example:


    Figure List Dialog

  3. Click the figure title to view the sheets in the figure.
  4. Hover over the name of the sheet that you want to download. These options appear:


  5. Click the Download Sheet icon.
    The sheet is downloaded.
