Delete Attachment

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

Note: It is only possible to delete attachments that were added directly with Pinpoint Viewer. You must also have all permissions enabled for Can Upload Supplement.

If you have proper permissions, do these steps to delete an existing attachment:

  1. Find the attachment you want to delete from the document in the Attachments pane.
    Refer to View Attachment.
  2. Click the delete attachment icon to the right of the attachment you want to delete.
    A delete confirmation pop-up appears.
  3. Click the red Remove button.

Pinpoint removes the attachment from the document. If you deleted an attachment with Pinpoint Viewer online, it is also deleted for all other users of Pinpoint Viewer online and a synchronization job can delete it from Pinpoint Standalone. If you deleted the attachment in Pinpoint Standalone however, it is not deleted from Pinpoint Viewer online. Additionally, the attachment is deleted for Pinpoint Mobile users if they do a manual synchronization.
