Delete Figure/Sheet

Not applicable for Pinpoint Standalone Light.

Deleting a figure/figure sheet removes it permanently from the document. Before deleting a figure/figure sheet, you should remove all attachments, annotations, and references to it or move those to a different figure/figure sheet. Once you delete the figure/figure sheet, these elements will no longer display correctly.

Users with the Can author content permission can delete figures and figure sheets. Deleting figures or sheets is available from the Figure List dialog.

To open the Figure List dialog, do these steps:
  1. Enter authoring mode. Refer to Enter Authoring Mode.
  2. From the Content pane header, select the Figure List icon.
    The Figure List dialog appears. For example:
