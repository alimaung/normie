# ATS (Antrag Teile und Stoffe/Consumables and Materials Supply Request) workflow

5 Stage Lifecycle:

1. Applicant stage [APP] (initial)
2. Hazards stage [UUB]
3. Environmental stage [UWS]
4. Health and Safety [HSE] stage
5. Materials Lab [LAB] stage
6. Standardization Office stage [NRM] (final)
