About MLC Check

The Maritime Labour Convention (MLC) Check tool allows you to search for substances and their status according to MLC 132 regulations. You can:

  • Manual Search: Enter CAS numbers, EC numbers, or substance names directly
  • SDS Upload: Upload a Safety Data Sheet to automatically extract and search chemical identifiers
  • Automated Analysis: Uses SDS detection and CAS extraction to identify relevant substances

The results will show the substance's status (prohibited, restricted, etc.) and any specific requirements or details according to MLC regulations.
